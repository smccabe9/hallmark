The A answer formula

Prof. Navarro-Farr: proper terms, defined, with examples, and explicit. Use these four steps for every short answer.

  1. DefineOpen with the key term and a one-sentence definition.
  2. ExplainSay how it works or why it matters, step by step. She won’t infer anything.
  3. ExampleA specific case: blue jeans, atlatls, CWAM, the film.
  4. ConnectEnd with one sentence tying it to context, chronology or stewardship.
